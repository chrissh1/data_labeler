Before you begin

We’re studying how people interpret emotions in short messages. Your labels help us compare human judgments with existing dataset labels.

  1. You’ll receive up to five tweets. Choose the emotion most strongly expressed by each author.
  2. If more than one fits, choose the closest. Base your answer only on the text.
  3. Choose a label, then select Next. Use the numbered buttons to review any tweet.
  4. When all tweets are labeled, Submit labels replaces Next. Nothing is saved to the results until you submit the whole batch.

After submitting, you can stop or label another batch. Use the same email to return to your remaining tweets. We save a coded participant identifier, not your email.
