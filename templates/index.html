We’re studying how people interpret emotions in short messages. Your labels help us compare human judgments with existing dataset labels.

How to label

  1. Read the message and choose the emotion most strongly expressed by its author. Base your answer only on the text.
  2. If several emotions seem possible, choose the closest fit. Use your own judgment; you don’t need to guess the original label.
  3. Select Next to continue. You can go Back to change an answer. After the fifth message, select Submit labels.
